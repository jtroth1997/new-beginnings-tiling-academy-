The complete programme

Ten days.
A lifetime skill.

A structured journey from understanding your tools to completing and presenting a fully tiled bathroom area.

Ask about the course
Meet the team, tour the academy, understand your tools, adhesives, grouts, trowels, laser levels and how to set out your first wall.
Measure areas accurately, prepare walls, price jobs, cut with a grinder, drill holes and work neatly around obstacles.
Set out bays, work with pipe cut-outs and diamond patterns, fit trims, form mitres and complete a full window wall.
Prepare the floor, choose adhesives and trowels, install matting, set out and tile the floor correctly.
Measure and price your own bay, calculate materials, set out and fully tile a bathroom wall and floor, grout, finish and present your work.
